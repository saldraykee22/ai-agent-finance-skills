# Research Production Workflow

## Standard Pipelines

- Daily BIST and crypto briefing: regime, calendar, watchlist, alerts, abnormal moves, pre-trade candidates.
- Weekly portfolio review: performance, risk, cash, rebalance, thesis updates, journal lessons.
- Event note: disclosure, filing, earnings, unlock, macro event, regulatory change, corporate action.
- Watchlist refresh: screen, rank, rotate, score, alert, thesis register.
- Decision memo: specialist analysis, signal score, committee challenge, pre-trade gate.

## Production Gates

No report should be finalized until facts, calculations, scenarios, uncertainties, and next checks are explicit.
