# Breadth Internals Workflow

## Indicators

- BIST: advancing/declining stocks, sector breadth, new highs/lows, XU030 versus XU100, equal-weight behavior, volume breadth, limit-up/limit-down clustering.
- Crypto: BTC dominance, ETH/BTC, altcoin breadth, sector breadth, stablecoin liquidity, exchange volume breadth, top-heavy market cap concentration.
- Watchlist: percentage above moving averages, breakout participation, relative strength distribution, liquidity breadth.

## Interpretation

Classify breadth as expanding, stable, narrowing, divergent, or capitulating. Connect the classification to watchlist aggressiveness and cash deployment pace.
