# Crypto Narrative Rotation Workflow

## Narrative Evidence

- Market evidence: relative strength, volume share, market cap share, BTC and ETH pair strength, exchange breadth.
- Liquidity evidence: stablecoin supply, CEX depth, DEX pool depth, spreads, funding, open interest only as positioning context.
- Fundamental evidence: active users, revenue or fees, TVL quality, retention, protocol integrations, developer activity, app usage.
- Supply evidence: float, FDV, unlock calendar, emissions, treasury sales, staking rewards, bridge or wrapped supply.
- Crowding evidence: extreme social heat, leverage, thin liquidity, repeated influencer driven pumps, rapid listing cascades.

## Scoring

Rank narratives by momentum, durability, liquidity, tokenomics, catalyst timing, and downside asymmetry. End with spot watchlist candidates and invalidation checks.
