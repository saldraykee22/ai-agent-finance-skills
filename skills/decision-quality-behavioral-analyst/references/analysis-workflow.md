# Decision Quality and Behavioral Workflow

## Bias checklist

- FOMO: entering because price moved, not because setup confirmed.
- Revenge: trying to recover a loss quickly.
- Overconfidence: size too large relative to evidence.
- Confirmation bias: seeking only supporting data.
- Anchoring: fixating on cost basis or old target.
- Loss aversion: refusing to exit after thesis break.
- Endowment effect: valuing held assets more than new alternatives.
- Recency bias: extrapolating the latest candle/news too far.
- Thesis drift: changing reason for holding after original thesis failed.

## Decision process

1. State the decision: buy, wait, add, trim, exit, hold.
2. List evidence for and against.
3. Check policy fit and position lifecycle stage.
4. Identify emotional pressure and time pressure.
5. Compare alternatives:
   - Do nothing.
   - Smaller size.
   - Wait for trigger.
   - Trim instead of exit.
   - Exit and revisit later.
6. Convert into rule or journal note.
