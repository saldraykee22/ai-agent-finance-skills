## Interpretation

- `Ready`: evidence is current, trigger exists, liquidity works, invalidation is clear, portfolio fit is acceptable.
- `Watch`: thesis is interesting but trigger, data, or valuation is incomplete.
- `Wait for Trigger`: setup is valid but price, volume, event, or liquidity confirmation is missing.
- `Reduce`: thesis weakened, risk budget breached, or better alternatives dominate.
- `Reject`: data conflict, poor liquidity, broken thesis, excessive downside, or no edge.

## Weighting Guidance

For BIST and crypto spot, do not let a single valuation or narrative argument dominate liquidity, timing, invalidation, and portfolio concentration checks.
