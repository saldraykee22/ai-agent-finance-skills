# Corporate Actions Workflow

## Action checklist

- Dividends: gross/net amount, currency, ex-date, record date, payment date, yield, withholding if relevant.
- Splits/bonus issues: ratio, effective date, adjusted share count, chart adjustment.
- Rights issues: subscription price, ratio, theoretical ex-rights price, dilution, use of proceeds.
- Buybacks: authorization size, execution, average price, cancellation/treasury treatment.
- M&A/tender: offer price, consideration mix, conditions, approvals, timeline, spread.
- Spin-off: distribution ratio, pro forma financials, index treatment, cost basis if relevant.
- Delisting: offer terms, last trading date, liquidity impact.
- Token unlock/emission/burn: amount, percent of circulating supply, recipient class, vesting, market liquidity.
- Index changes: announcement date, effective date, passive flow, liquidity impact.

## Process

1. Verify official source and dates.
2. Identify mechanical effects:
   - Share count, price adjustment, per-share metrics, market cap, EV, supply.
3. Identify economic effects:
   - Cash outflow/inflow, dilution, ownership transfer, sell pressure, passive flow, signaling.
4. Update analysis:
   - Adjust charts, valuation, yield, historical returns, and liquidity assumptions.
5. Scenario test:
   - Smooth execution, adverse flow, cancellation/delay, regulatory issue.
