# Sector and Industry Workflow

## Sector KPI map

- Banks: NIM, loan/deposit growth, NPL, cost of risk, CET1, ROE, deposits, duration.
- Insurance: combined ratio, solvency, float, reserve adequacy, investment yield.
- Software: ARR, NRR, churn, CAC payback, gross margin, SBC, RPO.
- Semiconductors/tech hardware: backlog, inventory, gross margin, capex cycle, end-market mix.
- Defense: backlog, book-to-bill, budget cycle, export approvals, working capital.
- Energy: production, reserves, realized prices, lifting cost, hedges, sustaining capex.
- Aviation: load factor, yield, RASK/CASK, fuel, fleet, FX debt.
- Retail: same-store sales, traffic, basket, gross margin, inventory turns, leases.
- Industrials: order intake, backlog, utilization, working capital, pricing power.
- Autos: deliveries, ASP, incentives, mix, battery cost, financing, emissions regulation.
- Real estate/REITs: NAV, occupancy, rent growth, cap rates, debt maturity, FFO/AFFO.
- Healthcare/biotech: pipeline, trial phase, patent cliff, payer mix, approval timeline.
- Telecom: ARPU, churn, capex intensity, spectrum, leverage.
- Mining/metals: grade, cash cost, reserves, capex, royalties, commodity exposure.

## Process

1. Define sector and sub-sector.
2. Build peer set by business model, geography, size, and listing venue.
3. Select KPIs and valuation metrics.
4. Compare growth, margin, balance sheet, returns, governance, and cycle sensitivity.
5. Identify sector catalysts: regulation, commodity prices, rates, currency, demand cycle.
6. Rank names by quality, valuation, momentum, and risk.
