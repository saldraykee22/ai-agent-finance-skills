# Distressed Balance Sheet Workflow

## Source hierarchy

- Latest financial statements and notes.
- Debt maturity table, covenants, credit facilities.
- Company disclosures, rating agency notes, bond prices/yields when available.
- KAP/SEC/EQS filings and going concern language.

## Analysis process

1. Identify cash, debt, short-term debt, net debt, interest expense, and maturities.
2. Calculate:
   - Net debt/EBITDA.
   - Interest coverage.
   - Current ratio.
   - FCF after interest and capex.
   - Liquidity runway.
3. Check stress triggers:
   - Covenant breach.
   - Refinancing wall.
   - Negative FCF.
   - Working capital squeeze.
   - FX mismatch.
   - Auditor/going concern warning.
4. Equity impact:
   - Dilution.
   - Asset sale.
   - Debt restructuring.
   - Default.
   - Turnaround upside.

## Watchpoints

- Cheap multiples may be traps when equity is an option on refinancing.
- Short-term debt matters more when rates rise or FX weakens.
- Inventory/receivables quality can hide liquidity stress.
- For BIST, FX liabilities and local rates can dominate operating performance.
