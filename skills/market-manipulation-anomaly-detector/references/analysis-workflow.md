# Anomaly Detection Workflow

## Red Flags

- Price move without verified catalyst.
- Volume multiple far above baseline in a low liquidity asset.
- Wide spread, thin book, and rapid reversals.
- Repeated upper or lower limit behavior with little depth.
- Social hype preceding liquidity or exchange listings.
- Large holder, treasury, or unlock wallet movement near price spike.
- Abnormal move before filing, KAP disclosure, earnings, or exchange notice.
- CEX price diverges from DEX or other venues without clear reason.

## Classification

Use `Low`, `Medium`, `High`, or `Critical` anomaly risk. Include what facts would change the classification.
