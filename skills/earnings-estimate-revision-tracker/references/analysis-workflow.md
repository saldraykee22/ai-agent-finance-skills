# Estimate Revision Workflow

## Fields

Revenue, gross margin, EBITDA, EBIT, net income, EPS, free cash flow, net debt, capex, segment KPIs, guidance range, consensus date, analyst count, revision direction, surprise history, peer read-through.

## Setup Classifications

- `Low bar`: expectations cut, price weak, fundamentals stabilizing.
- `High bar`: expectations raised, price extended, little room for miss.
- `Mixed`: estimates diverge or key line items disagree.
- `Stale`: old consensus, limited analyst coverage, unreliable bar.
- `Reset`: guidance or macro shock forces numbers lower.
