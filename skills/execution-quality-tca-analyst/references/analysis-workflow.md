# Execution Quality Workflow

## Required Trade Fields

Trade ID, asset, venue, side, planned quantity, filled quantity, order type, limit or market, planned price, arrival price, fill prices, timestamps, fees, FX conversion, spread estimate, benchmark price, and notes.

## Metrics

- Slippage: fill price versus arrival or planned price.
- Spread cost: executed price versus mid price when available.
- VWAP/TWAP difference: fill versus intraday execution benchmark.
- Market impact: adverse movement during and after order.
- Opportunity cost: missed fill or partial fill cost.
- Behavioral cost: rule deviation, chase, panic, or ignored liquidity plan.

## Review Standard

End with one execution improvement that can be used in the next pre-trade checklist.
