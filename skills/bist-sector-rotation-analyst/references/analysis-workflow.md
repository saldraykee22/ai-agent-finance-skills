# BIST Sector Rotation Workflow

## Sector Drivers

- Banks: rates, net interest margin, asset quality, regulation, capital adequacy, CDS, TRY liquidity.
- Holdings: NAV discount, portfolio catalysts, buybacks, balance sheet currency mix.
- Aviation: passenger traffic, fuel, FX, capacity, tourism, fleet, lease costs.
- Defense: order backlog, export permits, government budget, margin mix.
- Energy: commodity prices, regulation, tariffs, FX debt, generation mix.
- Retail: inflation pass-through, wage pressure, same-store sales, working capital.
- Industrials and exporters: EUR demand, TRY, input costs, order backlog.
- Real estate: rates, inventory, regulation, leverage, valuation appraisals.
- Technology and software: recurring revenue, FX revenue, margin scalability, customer concentration.

## Rotation Score

Score each sector on relative strength, breadth, volume confirmation, catalyst density, valuation gap, earnings revision, liquidity, and crowding risk. Prefer a ranked table plus a short interpretation.
