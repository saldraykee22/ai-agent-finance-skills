# Personal Investment Policy Workflow

## Default profile

- Primary markets: crypto spot and BIST equities.
- Secondary markets: US equities, German equities, FX, commodities, and funds only when relevant to macro, hedge, or opportunity comparison.
- Default instrument: spot only.
- Default objective: process-consistent opportunity selection, portfolio survival, and repeatable decision quality.
- Default style: evidence-based, scenario-aware, source-verified, no generic boilerplate caveats.

## Policy sections

1. Universe:
   - Primary: BIST listed equities, liquid crypto spot pairs.
   - Secondary: major US/Germany equities, ETFs, FX, commodities.
   - Exclusions: instruments with unclear identity, poor liquidity, unresolved source conflicts, or unsupported corporate/token action data.
2. Time horizon:
   - Tactical: days to weeks.
   - Position: weeks to months.
   - Strategic: multi-month thesis.
3. Risk budget:
   - Define maximum capital at risk per idea.
   - Define maximum exposure by asset, sector, theme, currency, and exchange.
   - Require smaller sizing for low-liquidity BIST names, high-volatility altcoins, event risk, or unclear invalidation.
4. Entry requirements:
   - Verified identity and current data.
   - Thesis, catalyst, trigger, invalidation, liquidity check, and portfolio impact.
5. Add/trim/exit rules:
   - Add only when thesis improves or price confirms with controlled risk.
   - Trim when exposure, valuation, or crowding exceeds plan.
   - Exit when thesis breaks, invalidation hits, liquidity changes, or better opportunity displaces it.
6. Review cadence:
   - After major price move, event, earnings/KAP, token unlock, macro shock, or rule breach.

## Decision gate

```text
Allowed:
- Fits universe, horizon, liquidity, data, risk, and thesis rules.

Conditionally allowed:
- Needs a trigger, source verification, smaller size, or event wait.

Rejected:
- No clear invalidation, poor liquidity, unresolved data conflict, excessive concentration, or outside spot policy.
```
