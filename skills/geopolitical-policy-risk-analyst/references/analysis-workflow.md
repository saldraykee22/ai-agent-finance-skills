# Geopolitical and Policy Risk Workflow

## Risk categories

- Elections and policy regime change.
- Sanctions and trade restrictions.
- War/conflict and energy security.
- Monetary/fiscal policy credibility.
- Capital controls, market access, exchange restrictions.
- Sector regulation: banks, energy, telecom, defense, crypto exchanges.
- Tax and transaction rule changes.

## Transmission channels

- FX and rates.
- Inflation and margins.
- Commodity prices.
- Liquidity and capital flows.
- Sector revenue/cost/regulation.
- Investor risk premium.
- Exchange/custody access for crypto.

## Process

1. Verify event status and timeline.
2. Identify affected assets and sectors.
3. Map first-order and second-order effects.
4. Separate immediate price reaction from durable thesis change.
5. Translate to spot portfolio:
   - Reduce exposure.
   - Avoid illiquid assets.
   - Wait for clarity.
   - Hedge via cash/currency/asset mix if relevant.
