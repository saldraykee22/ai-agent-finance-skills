# Position Lifecycle Workflow

## Stages

1. Idea:
   - What is the edge, catalyst, and timeframe?
2. Watchlist:
   - What trigger would make it actionable?
3. Entry:
   - What price/condition confirms entry?
4. Add:
   - Add only on thesis confirmation, improved risk/reward, or planned scaling.
5. Hold:
   - Monitor thesis, price behavior, catalysts, and portfolio exposure.
6. Trim:
   - Trim on concentration, valuation stretch, catalyst pass, liquidity deterioration, or partial target.
7. Exit:
   - Exit on thesis break, invalidation, better opportunity, risk regime change, or planned target.
8. Post-mortem:
   - Was the result due to process, market regime, execution, or luck?

## Required position card

- Asset, venue, currency.
- Thesis.
- Catalyst.
- Entry trigger.
- Invalidation.
- Initial size and max size.
- Add/trim rules.
- Liquidity/execution notes.
- Review date and event calendar.
