# Earnings Call and Transcript Workflow

## Source hierarchy

- Company transcript or official webcast.
- Earnings release, investor presentation, KAP/SEC/EQS filings.
- Company IR materials and prior-period call materials.
- Reputable transcript providers only when official transcript is unavailable.

## Analysis process

1. Identify company, period, reporting currency, and release date.
2. Compare current call to prior commentary:
   - Guidance language.
   - Demand environment.
   - Margin bridge.
   - Pricing, volume, FX, cost inflation.
   - Capex, working capital, debt, cash flow.
3. Separate prepared remarks from Q&A.
4. Flag Q&A pressure:
   - Repeated analyst concern.
   - Evasive answer.
   - Changed wording.
   - Pull-forward or delayed catalyst.
5. Map commentary to numbers:
   - Does management's narrative match financial statement evidence?
6. Translate into thesis impact:
   - Strengthened, unchanged, weakened, or unverified.

## Watchpoints

- Management tone can be promotional; anchor to numbers.
- One quarter should not override a multi-quarter trend without a mechanism.
- Guidance can be metric-specific; do not generalize.
- For BIST companies, presentations and KAP notes may substitute for call transcripts.
