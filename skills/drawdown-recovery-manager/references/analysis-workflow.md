# Drawdown Recovery Workflow

## Diagnosis

- Market regime: broad selloff, local BIST shock, crypto liquidity shock, FX move, rates shock.
- Portfolio structure: concentration, correlation, leverage if any, illiquidity, single narrative exposure.
- Position quality: broken thesis, early entry, poor invalidation, no exit discipline.
- Execution: slippage, chasing, oversized order, poor venue, wrong session.
- Behavior: revenge trading, averaging down without rule, FOMO, refusal to trim.

## Recovery Modes

- `Pause`: no new risk until data and journal review are done.
- `Stabilize`: reduce fragile exposure and rebuild cash.
- `Selective redeploy`: only best-ranked, liquid, pre-trade-passed ideas.
- `Normal risk`: resume after process rules are restored.
