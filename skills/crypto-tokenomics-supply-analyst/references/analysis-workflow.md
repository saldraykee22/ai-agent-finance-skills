# Crypto Tokenomics and Supply Workflow

## Source hierarchy

- Official tokenomics docs and vesting schedule.
- Verified contract and block explorer.
- Project governance/treasury disclosures.
- Reputable unlock trackers and data aggregators.
- Exchange supply pages only after contract identity is verified.

## Process

1. Verify token:
   - Name, ticker, chain, contract, decimals, wrapped/bridged status.
2. Establish supply:
   - Circulating supply.
   - Liquid float.
   - Total/max supply.
   - FDV and market cap.
3. Map allocation:
   - Team, investors, ecosystem, treasury, community, staking, liquidity incentives.
4. Analyze unlocks:
   - Date, size, percent of circulating supply, recipient, historical behavior, market depth.
5. Analyze emissions/burns:
   - Net inflation, staking yield, burn rate, fee capture.
6. Translate to spot:
   - Avoid before unlock.
   - Wait for post-unlock absorption.
   - Size smaller.
   - Thesis unaffected if unlock is small/absorbed.
