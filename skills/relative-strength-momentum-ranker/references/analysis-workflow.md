# Relative Strength Ranking Workflow

## Suggested Fields

| Asset | Market | Benchmark | 1W RS | 1M RS | 3M RS | Trend | Volume | Drawdown | Liquidity | Rank | Action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

## Scoring Ideas

- Reward persistent outperformance across multiple windows.
- Reward trend with volume expansion and shallow pullbacks.
- Penalize parabolic one-day moves unless catalyst and liquidity support them.
- Penalize instruments with upcoming dilution, unlocks, rights issues, or weak market depth.
- Compare crypto tokens against BTC, ETH, stablecoin pair, and narrative basket when possible.
- Compare BIST equities against XU100, sector index, and liquidity peers.
